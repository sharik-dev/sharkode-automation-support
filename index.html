Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Sharkode Automation is a private tool used only by the Instagram account @make.money.with.ai.nowwwww to publish its own Reels and to answer people who comment on them. It is not offered to anyone else, has no users of its own and shows no ads.

What it reads

When you comment on one of our Reels, the tool reads that comment through the official Instagram API: its text, its identifier, the time it was posted and your public username. It reads nothing else from your profile, and nothing from posts that are not ours.

What it does with it

If your comment contains the keyword announced in the video (for example “JUMP”), the tool sends you one private message with the links that were promised, and may reply under your comment to say so. This uses Instagram's “private reply” feature: one message per comment, within seven days. You only receive further messages if you reply yourself.

What it keeps

To avoid messaging you twice, it stores the comment identifier, your username, the keyword and the date in a file on our own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. These records are deleted after 90 days.

Deleting your data

Delete your comment, or email us at the address below with your Instagram username, and we will remove every record about you within 30 days.
